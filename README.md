A·Dent Dental Clinics – Homepage Redesign

1. Website Overview

Business Purpose: Provide comprehensive information regarding dental procedures (implants, cosmetic dentistry, specialist treatments) and streamline lead generation for international patients seeking dental tourism packages in Bulgaria.

Target Audience: International patients (primarily from the United Kingdom) and domestic patients in Bulgaria requiring specialist dental treatment and all-inclusive packages (incorporating accommodation, airport transfers, and remote consultations).

Homepage Design Direction: Establish trust, highlight clinical credentials, showcase genuine differentiating features (in-house lab, on-site accommodation, specialist lead surgeon), and simplify direct consultation inquiries.

2. Main Improvements

Clear Integration of International Patient Logistics Services

Previous Issue: Fragmented presentation of travel support made it difficult for UK patients to understand how treatment abroad works.

Fix Implemented: Dedicated homepage sections prominently feature end-to-end dental tourism support, including airport transfers, accommodation located directly above the Gotse Delchev clinic, and remote consultations.

Prominent Display of Clinical Expertise and Verified Claims

Previous Issue: Lack of immediate visual focus on clinical credibility, leading to lower conversion confidence for high-value treatments (e.g., implants).

Fix Implemented: Hero and team sections directly surface verified company credentials: Lead Surgeon Dr. Ramiz Arnaudov (16+ years experience, 8,000+ placed implants), Bulgarian Dental Chamber registration, and social proof (4.8 Google rating across 168 reviews).

Structured Service Categorization & Dual-Location Clarity

Previous Issue: Confusion regarding clinic operations, service scope, and laboratory capabilities.

Fix Implemented: Standardized layout separating the core clinical services (Implants, Cosmetic, Specialist) from the state-of-the-art in-house dental laboratory (Exocad, Zirconia, 3D printing), with clear address listings for both the Gotse Delchev and Bansko clinics.

3. Design & UX Improvements

Visual Presentation: Clean, medical-grade aesthetic utilizing high-contrast typography, clinical iconography, and prominent call-to-action buttons for immediate contact.

Layout & Hierarchy: Intuitive sectioning transitioning from hero value propositions to credentials, treatment categories, international patient travel flows, team profiles, and dual-location contact footers.

User Experience (UX): Streamlined conversion paths allowing users to initiate direct communication via phone (+359 87 633 3382), WhatsApp, or online consultation forms seamlessly.

Consistency: Standardized service cards and uniform visual indicators for treatment packages, laboratory capabilities, and clinical team details across the page.
