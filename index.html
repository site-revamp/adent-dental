Understand your treatment costs

Clarity before you make a decision.

Dental treatment costs depend on the treatment required, the number of teeth involved, clinical findings and the materials or procedures included in your plan.

Before confirming travel, ask for a written treatment plan that explains the proposed procedures, itemised costs, package inclusions and any possible additional expenses.

  • A clear explanation of the proposed treatment
  • Itemised costs and package inclusions
  • Appointment and visit expectations

Informed decisions and continued care

Know what happens beyond your appointment.

Choosing dental treatment abroad means understanding both the treatment itself and the arrangements that follow. Patients should have the opportunity to ask about clinical responsibility, professional registration, records, warranties and follow-up care.

Before proceeding, request written details relevant to your own treatment and clarify how to contact the clinic if you have questions after returning home.

Ask about aftercare →

Questions worth asking

  • Who is responsible for my treatment? Confirm the treating clinician and their professional registration.
  • What happens after I return home? Ask about follow-up arrangements and how to contact the clinic.
  • Can I receive my dental records? Clarify how copies of relevant records and treatment details can be provided.
  • What does the warranty cover? Request the written terms, exclusions and conditions that apply to your treatment.

Professional registration, patient rights and applicable standards differ between countries. Verify the relevant information independently and ask the clinic for current documentation.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Planning treatment abroad can raise practical and clinical questions. Use these as a starting point for your conversation with the A·Dent team.

Contact A·Dent
Where are A·Dent's clinics located?

A·Dent lists clinic locations in Gotse Delchev and Bansko, Bulgaria. Contact the clinic to confirm the location relevant to your proposed treatment.

Can international patients contact A·Dent before travelling?

A·Dent provides contact routes for international enquiries. You can contact the team to ask about consultation arrangements, information requirements and the next steps before planning a visit.

Will I need more than one visit?

That depends on the treatment and your clinical circumstances. Some procedures, including certain implant treatment plans, may involve multiple visits separated by a healing period. Ask your clinician to explain the expected stages before booking travel.

Does A·Dent provide accommodation or transfer support?

A·Dent describes accommodation and transport support for international patients. Confirm availability, eligibility, costs and exactly what is included in your own arrangements.

How can I understand the full cost of treatment?

Request a written, itemised treatment plan. It should explain the proposed procedures, what the quoted price includes, any exclusions and circumstances that could affect the final cost.

What should I ask about aftercare?

Ask who to contact after treatment, how follow-up is arranged, how to obtain your records and what written warranty terms apply. You should also discuss what to do if you have a concern after returning home.
